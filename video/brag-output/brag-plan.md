# Grizzly Energy: brag plan (v2)

**What it is:** a halal energy drink made in Pakistan, with added Zamzam water, in six flavors. Tagline: "Fuel your wild side."
**Who it's for:** people who want energy from natural caffeine (green coffee beans), electrolytes from sea salt and B vitamins, and who care that it is halal certified.
**What sets it apart:** added Zamzam water in every can, and halal certification, from a Pakistani brand with a wild, roaring-bear identity.
**Most impressive claim (from the data):** "Every can is made with added Zamzam water."
**Visual hook:** the website's own night stage (mountains, moon, mist), with the bear from the label roaring up behind the can in a flash of light.
**Real thing shown:** the site's actual WebGL can (geometry, label art, brushed-metal shader, condensation), its Stage backdrop (night, flavor colour field, colour-wave wipe, studio), its roar and Zamzam-pool moments, its fonts (Libre Franklin 900 italic, Geist, Geist Mono, Amiri) and its accent colours per flavor.
**Tone:** `cinematic`: trailer scale, big type, dramatic wipes, but restrained and premium (the site's own direction: futuristic, minimal, physical, product-first).
**Share caption:** "Six flavors. Natural caffeine, electrolytes from sea salt, B vitamins and added Zamzam water. Halal certified, made in Pakistan. Fuel your wild side."

## Visual identity (from the site)

- Night stage: black-blue sky, moon, ridge-line mountains in fog, wet floor reflections, low mist.
- Flavor chapters: full-bleed colour field in the flavor accent, the colour wave expanding from the can on every change.
- Accents: Blue Raspberry #1f9beb, Mango Fuego #f29a1d, Watermelon #e8433a, Strawberry Kiwi #e32b56, Peach #f0843a, Blackout Berry #9b5de5. Zamzam: gold on dawn water.
- Type: Libre Franklin 900 italic, uppercase, tight; Geist 300 for body; Geist Mono with wide tracking for indices and chips; Amiri for زمزم.
- Frame furniture: corner brackets and the "01 / 06" index chip from the site.
